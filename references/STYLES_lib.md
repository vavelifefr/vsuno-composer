## Electronic forms

| Style | Typical tempo / groove | Defining palette | Voice / development |
|---|---|---|---|
| Techno | 125–145, steady machine pulse | 909/808-like kit, mono bass, ostinato/stabs | Instrumental or sparse voice; filters, percussion, plateau, withdrawal/return |
| Minimal / deep techno | 115–135, sparse pulse | Sub, clicks/stabs, evolving pad | Small changes and space; no compulsory big EDM build |
| Acid techno / house | 120–145 | 303-like resonant bass, 909/808-like drums | Filter movement; optional short chants |
| Early-1990s rave | 125–160, straight pulse and/or chopped breaks | Hoover, piano stabs, sub, machine/sampler palette | Sparse shouts/chant or instrumental; rhythmic/texture contrasts |
| Hardcore | 150–190 | Distorted kick, sub, hoover | Abrupt returns and forceful accents |
| House | 118–130, quarter-note kick/offbeat hats | 909/707-like drums, syncopated bass, piano/organ | Soulful hook or instrumental; recurring groove |
| Deep house | 110–125, softened swing | Round bass, Rhodes-like keys, pad | Close voice and spacious responses |
| Tech house | 122–132, punchy pulse | Rolling bass, short percussion/stabs | Sparse hook, filter/stops |
| Disco / funky house | 115–130 | Syncopated guitar, bass, keys/strings | Soulful answers and filtering; original sample-like parts |
| Italo disco | 100–125 | Octave/sequenced bass, LinnDrum/707/808-like kit, Juno/Jupiter/Prophet-like synths | Melodic refrain and synth interlude |
| Eurodance | 125–150 | 909-like kit, offbeat bass, M1-like piano/organ, bright synth | Strong vocal hook, optional rap; avoid stock lyric clichés |
| Trance | 130–145 | Rolling/offbeat bass, arpeggio, broad lead/pads; JP-8000-like only for suitable era | Sustained/breathy voice when wanted; longer anticipation/return |
| Psytrance | 135–150, tight kick/bass | FM/resonant tones and FX | Minimal words; no compulsory pop chorus |
| Jungle | 155–175, sliced breaks | Sub/Reese, sampler-like pads | Own short vocal cuts; changing break density |
| Drum and bass | 165–180 | Breaks, sub/Reese, synth/sample-like palette | Hook to full song; half-time contrast and return |
| Liquid drum and bass | 165–180, rolling | Warm sub, Rhodes/piano/pads | Soulful melody and harmonic color |
| Big beat / breaks | 115–140, heavy breaks | Synth/electric bass, distorted stabs | Bursts, stops and returns |
| Electro | 110–135, syncopated machine rhythm | 808-like kit, short bass, synthetic lead | Optional vocoder/robotic voice; sparse interplay |
| Synthpop | 90–130, machine song groove | Synth bass, Juno/Prophet/Jupiter/DX7-like palette | Clear lead, verse/chorus; no compulsory EDM drop |
| Synthwave | 75–120 | Analog-like bass/arpeggio, warm synth, electronic drums | Instrumental or melodic voice; space/gated textures without mandatory lo-fi |
| Ambient | Free or slow | Drone, pads, fragments | Instrumental or intentional wordless voice; gradual/continuous development |

## Song and acoustic forms

| Style | Characteristic foundation | Voice / form |
|---|---|---|
| Pop | Flexible tempo/meter; drums, bass, piano/guitar/synth | Clear verse/chorus contrast; no perpetual maximum density |
| Ballad | Slow/moderate, 4/4 or 6/8/rubato; piano, guitar, optional strings | Intimate/open phrasing; long verses, refrain or no separate chorus |
| Folk | Chosen tradition/pulse; guitar, fiddle, accordion, flute/percussion as relevant | Narrative form; avoid an arbitrary mixture of unrelated traditions |
| Blues | Shuffle or straight; guitar, bass, drums, piano/harmonica | Speech-like inflection/bends, call-response; twelve-bar and AAB are options |
| Funk | Syncopation and gaps; bass, muted guitar, clav/Rhodes/Hammond-like keys, live drums | Rhythmic phrases, chants/responses when appropriate; not constant unison |
| Disco | Four-on-the-floor, live bass/guitar, strings/brass/keys | Lead/backing interplay, refrain and instrumental passages |
| Rock | Guitar riff, bass, live drums, optional keys | Restrained/raspy/belted as needed; solo need not mean shredding |
| Soul / R&B | Backbeat, syncopation/space; bass, drums, keys/guitar and era-appropriate synth | Melody, purposeful runs/harmonies; distinguish retro and modern palette |
| Hip-hop / boom bap | Often 75–105, backbeat/swing; original sample-like texture, drums/bass/sparse keys | Rap and concise hook, internal rhyme and breathing space |
| Trap | Half-time feel, active hats, tuned 808-like bass, sparse harmony/claps | Rap/melodic voice, gaps between dense events |
| Cinematic action | Ostinato, percussion/hits, strings/brass/synth | Instrumental or intentional choir; thematic peak/release, no compulsory song chorus |

## Era and equipment

S08 records selected Roland dates: Jupiter-8 1981, SH-101 1982, Juno-106 1984, D-50 1987, JD-800 1991, JP-8000 1997. A strict early-1990s palette cannot use JP-8000 as period equipment; choose a compatible source or agree a modern reinterpretation. Do not assume LinnDrum in a 1970s production without evidence. A broad historical palette can be described through live/analog-like roles when exact gear is unverified.

Manufacturer dates do not prove use in a reference track. Gear is a desired timbre, not guaranteed device emulation. Preserve user's explicit instrument choices unless a conflict requires clarification. Reference identities remain in personal/references, outside final chunks.
