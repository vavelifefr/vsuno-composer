# Harmony supporting motif, bass and form

Use for harmonic support/development/voice leading, not as a mandatory chord worksheet. M02/M05 and S02/S10 in SOURCES_lib support concepts; original checks 2026-10-04. Prompt techniques are DESIGN, not guaranteed Suno controls.

Choose tonal center/mode, harmonic movement/rate, melody-bass relationship and meaningful section contrast. Exact chords are optional unless requested. Atonal textures need no invented tonic. The first chord alone does not establish key.

Tonic is the tonal reference; dominant describes a pull toward it; cadence is harmonic/melodic closure. Modal loops, blues and static grooves need not follow a classical dominant-tonic plan.

| Goal | Possible tool / dependency |
|---|---|
| Stability | Repeated loop/held harmony; rhythm/timbre/motif still develop |
| Arrival/expectation | Incomplete movement with placed or delayed resolution |
| Breathing space | Slower chord changes, sustained support and lower density |
| New meaning for a familiar motif | Reharmonization compatible with actual melody and bass |
| Continuity | Common tones or smooth inner motion, suitable register |
| Sustained tension | Pedal tone compatible with changing chords |
| Open ending | Cyclic return or deferred closure related to next entry |

Harmonic rhythm is the rate of chord changes, not BPM or melodic note speed. Four chord symbols do not automatically specify four bars. Check given durations against form.

At a melodic peak choose stability or intentional tension and its destination. Passing/neighbor tones are not automatically wrong. Analyze actual notes when supplied; a verbal contour permits intentions, not invented note analysis.

Bass may use root, inversion, pedal, passing motion or an independent pattern. Separate sub/upper-layer roles under MAIN_THEME; do not default to conflicting harmonic anchors.

Voice leading is motion of individual lines, including instruments. Voicing is register/order/spacing of chord tones; equal symbols allow different voicings. Common tones require actual shared notes. Small inner motion can smooth keys/pads; wide leaps remain stylistic options. Contrary/parallel motion and wider spacing need role/register compatibility; parallel motion is not a universal error.

Optional color: borrowed chords from a parallel mode without claiming modulation; secondary dominant with a purpose; extensions/suspensions distinguishing sus/add/maj7/dominant7; modulation as a tonal-center change, not merely a register/octave rise. Preserve unusual user harmony; disclose uncertain analysis.

Styles names tonal support/movement/harmonic rhythm/performance. Lyrics places significant holds, changes and returns. Example design: Style: D minor, a steady harmonic loop with slower changes in the bridge; Instruments: warm electric-piano voicings with smooth inner motion. A local [Keys: sustained chord, then return to the verse loop] is descriptive, not guaranteed syntax. Unknown/high chord notation follows the permission rules in [SUNO_TAGS_lib.md](SUNO_TAGS_lib.md), including maximum-Lyrics permission and explicit safer overrides.
