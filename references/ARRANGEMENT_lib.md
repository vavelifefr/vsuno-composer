# Arrangement: roles, space and groove

Deepens part assignment in COMPOSE, not a second workflow. M03/M04 checked 2026-10-04 support concepts; cards/examples are DESIGN.

| Role | Function / useful decisions |
|---|---|
| Foreground | Recognizable phrase/meaning; carrier, contour/rhythm/register/returns |
| Response/counterline | Support or contrast; placement, overlap and invariant core |
| Harmony | Support/color; register, attack/duration, change rate |
| Bass | Harmony/motion or main theme; anchors versus kick/motif |
| Rhythm | Pulse/accents; feel, main hits, boundary variation |
| Texture/FX | Space/event; functional placement and duration |

Not every part needs a hook; purposeful polyphony is valid. Instruments may change role by section. Styles states overall identity; Lyrics marks handoffs. Avoid simultaneously describing continuous solo and restrained backing without placement.

Register is pitch region; density includes active lines and event rate. Parts can compete through register, attack and continuous rhythm. Free space by changing register, simplifying rhythm, sustaining notes, placing responses in gaps or removing optional doubles. Preserve intentional unison/doubling. Do not give every layer wide stereo or claim exact EQ/routing of unheard audio.

Backing/choir needs source, relationship and entry under VOCAL_lib. New texture does not authorize changing locked voice assignment.

Groove combines pulse, accents, subdivisions and rests. Straight/swing/shuffle/half-time are not interchangeable BPM labels. Do not guess exact swing ratio for a reference.

Examples of selected relationships: bass locks to main kick accents; syncopated bass answers between kicks; muted guitar replies in bass gaps; vocal pickups lead into refrain; sparse accompaniment under rapid verse; short instrumental answers after held vocal endings. These are choices, not universal funk/rap/house formulas. A fill prepares a selected entry without masking the next phrase.

Within a detailed section, keep groove while varying a bass ending, one response, layer withdrawal or final phrase expansion. Plateau can use subtle movement; changing everything erodes recognition. Multiple bass roles belong in MAIN_THEME; voice leading in HARMONY. Words-only edits preserve the approved musical map.

Original role-handoff example, not a preset:

```text
[Intro]
[Synth: short rising motif with a held ending]
[Verse]
[Synth: brief replies between vocal phrases]
[Male Vocal: restrained singing]
[Instrumental Break]
[Synth: full motif takes the lead over the steady bass]
[Chorus]
[Synth: doubles the final hook phrase, then leaves space]
[Male Vocal: open, sustained singing]
```

A real song needs original words after vocal cues. These descriptions have estimated risk; they are not proof of exact doubling. During existing review, check foreground, response space, returns and compatible register/harmony/pulse. Styles gives palette/ongoing relationships; Lyrics marks handoffs/entries/removals. Compress optional ornaments first; do not turn the prompt into a DAW routing sheet or claim verified balance without audio.
