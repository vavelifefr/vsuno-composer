## Legacy and task-specific sources

[Suno Styles](https://sunostyles.com/), including best-suno-prompts, vocals, genre-prompts and styles, is third-party and was not rechecked for this edition. Read specific pages before using vocabulary/observations; never transfer claims of official support or guaranteed safety.

Research manufacturers, instrument documentation and musician/producer interviews for the actual task. MusicBrainz/Discogs can support release metadata, not automatically exact BPM/key/gear/arrangement.

## Evidence policy

OFFICIAL: Suno on its own capabilities. PRIMARY: author/manufacturer/technical document within its domain. SECONDARY: independent account checked against its basis. USER-LISTENING: the user's observation. LEGACY: inherited claim without fresh verification. INFERRED: conclusion with a named basis. UNKNOWN: no information.

A trustworthy URL does not turn estimated risk into a measured rate. Syntax support needs construction-specific evidence; failure frequency needs observations with conditions. Preserve scope/version/dates and disagreements rather than voting by copied articles. During research obey citation limits and write concise original conclusions, not extensive paraphrases of one source.
