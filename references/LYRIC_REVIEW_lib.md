# One final review: meaning, sound and performance

Use after writing or a meaningful revision; SKILL and flows refer to this same review. Read-only views require complete extraction and preserved roles, not literary rewriting. Compare against the brief/source. Do not output hidden reasoning, decorative scores or a checklist instead of the song.

| Pass | Criteria | Local repair |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning/language | Recognizable premise, section functions, coherent narrator/addressee/pronouns, useful progression, understandable imagery and natural speech | Restore premise and repair affected passage |
| Sound | Actual sound correspondences, natural stress, intended scheme, delivery-compatible density/breath groups, meaningful punctuation, compatible final hook variation | Repair linked rhyme/phrase/stanza |
| Performance/music | Every phrase/insertion has an owner; lead/backing/shared roles clear; motif/key consistent; permitted main-theme presentation/returns distributed; secondary/bass roles meaningful; words have space | Repair assignment/event, not add an effect catalogue |

Scale to a short chant; skip nonexistent lyrics in an instrumental but reject unsolicited voices. Preserve user lines, bans, structure and locks throughout.

For new/revised Styles check readable categories and concrete interactions. Merge for an explicitly short brief; do not reformat untouched Styles. Do not require secondary themes/multiple bass layers. A newline is not guaranteed inhalation. Without audio, claim description consistency rather than verified singing, balance or note alignment.

Check selected composer dependencies within the same passes: semantic versus melodic stress; recognizable motif; harmony/bass compatibility; foreground/response roles; prepared transitions. Read MAIN_THEME/HARMONY/ARRANGEMENT/COMPOSITION only as needed.

For a new or musically redesigned composition at any detail level, check [MAIN_THEME_lib.md](MAIN_THEME_lib.md#default-recurrence-at-every-detail-level): where the user permits a theme, include its allowed initial presentation; ordinarily include at least two later recognizable returns when recurrence is permitted, with flexible placement and optional Intro. Short-form exceptions depend on actual form, not low detail or compact field size. Explicit user prohibitions and locks take priority; do not force a theme or returns they disallow. Each permitted placement retains a concise shared core description; variations and vocal/rhythmic themes are valid. Recheck the final rendered map after compression. Preserve untouched-source boundaries in local words-only edits/read-only views; this review does not authorize rewriting their music or prove audio recurrence.

For a doubtful rhyme compare stressed vowel through the following sounds, natural stresses and precise internal positions. Use the English or Russian profile for each block, with its actual pronunciation, grammar and phrase demands; language and singer are independent. Identical spelling/repeated words do not prove a new perfect rhyme. ABAC has one paired A family, not four paired endings. Every ad-lib need not rhyme. Literal translation has no unrequested rhyme requirement; singable adaptation rechecks rhyme in the target language.

Repair: defect -> role/function -> small replacement -> dependent pair/stanza/voice check. Preserve successful sections. If local repair fails, revisit only that section's plan. Conflicting protected words/melody require agreement. A mode change does not authorize core-file writes.

When compressing, preserve words/premise/roles/defining events and compatible main-theme placements. Repeated core descriptions at different planned returns are essential, not redundant global prose to delete. Shorten cues and remove optional duplicates/FX first. Recount final Lyrics/Styles against their selected ceilings and hard caps (Lyrics 5000; Styles 1000) in UTF-16 after changes. Review profile-derived options and each adjacent exclusion against the brief; flexible Exclude counts never justify unrelated/conflicting items. Mechanical helper acceptance is not proof of rhyme, originality, all-language compliance or Suno performance.
