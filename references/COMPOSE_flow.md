# Grow a skeleton into a composition

Resolve each field's size/detail under [INTAKE_flow.md](INTAKE_flow.md) before drafting. Match description depth to the selected profile; do not add sections, instruments or words merely to fill it. Use Lyrics detail for automatic More Options and Exclude targets, preserving explicit values and unchanged revision settings.

1. Define the sound concept: principal style and blend roles, emotional trajectory and, when permitted, a recognizable main theme. Use MAIN_THEME_lib for its carrier, key/mode, contour/rhythm, register/articulation, mood, intensity and development. Reference traits must be identity-free. Choose compatible neighboring exclusions under NEGATIVE_STYLES_lib.
2. Select sections, order and purpose under COMPOSITION_lib, respecting user form. Where the user permits a main theme, plan its initial presentation under [MAIN_THEME_lib.md](MAIN_THEME_lib.md#default-recurrence-at-every-detail-level), and plan at least two later recognizable returns when recurrence is permitted, even at basic detail. The LLM chooses locations; Intro is optional. Genuinely very short forms may use fewer returns without extension. Explicit user prohibitions and locks take priority; do not force a theme or returns they disallow. Minimal and through-composed designs need no forced Chorus. Set meaningful boundary changes and linking transitions; choose relevant phrase architecture inside sections. This refines one skeleton, not a second mandatory plan.
3. Assign drums/bass/lead/harmony/voices/FX. Describe important sources, patterns and actions: enter, withdraw, return, change. Select only useful attributes. If the user permits a main theme, define its invariant core and variations; secondary themes answer/support/contrast when useful. Separate chosen bass layers by role and coordinated entries, not quantity.
4. If words are needed, follow LYRICS_flow for section meaning, rhythm and rhyme. PROSODY_lib guides meaningful breath groups without sacrificing stress, rhyme or locks. Map multiple voices before assigning each vocal block/change. Give dense rap or speech-like singing space; instrumental responses should support the phrase, not appear after every line.
5. Reconcile global Styles with local Lyrics. A finale-only instrument cannot also be the continuous foundation. Place delivery changes clearly. Convert mood into rhythm, register, harmony, density and articulation.
6. Render for Suno: global identity/performance in Styles; local events in Lyrics; controls in MOREOPTIONS. Use multiline Styles categories under CHUNK_tpl. When the user permits a theme, keep its brief identity in Styles even at basic detail, merging into an existing category when compact. A section header precedes short music cues, then separate performed lines. At each planned presentation/return, repeat a concise description of the recognizable core, with useful variation if selected; a bare theme returns reference is insufficient. Do not bury hook words in an instruction. Place meaningful motif/bass interactions; a particular chorus may contrast if required returns are placed elsewhere. Distribute detailed events instead of one long command paragraph.
7. Review and compress by priority: constraints, subject/hook, section function, defining entries/patterns/contrasts/returns. Retain the compatible main-theme presentation and distributed returns; shorten their wording rather than deleting later placements. Follow explicit user prohibitions and locks if they rule out the theme or its recurrence. Remove optional global duplication, similar adjectives and FX. Rewrite an overlong section coherently rather than cutting its ending, then review the final recurrence map and counts.

Scale the musical design to the brief and each field's selected detail; description depth does not automatically increase composition complexity. Use [ARRANGEMENT_lib.md](../references/ARRANGEMENT_lib.md) for space/groove/roles and [HARMONY_lib.md](../references/HARMONY_lib.md) for support/voice leading. Plain loops and literal motif repeats are valid. Do not ask delegated harmonic decisions back or force a technique catalogue.

Reconcile only chosen dependencies: motif versus harmony/bass; foreground versus space; transition versus next entry; vocal peak versus semantic accent. This is the same reconciliation and final LYRIC_REVIEW, not extra full audits.

For detail, retain a compact section map: function, emotional/lyrical contribution, voice/language, phrasing, rhyme/repetition, insertion and motif state. A short task needs fewer decisions and no user-facing table. Optional state stores final decisions under PERSONAL_spec, not hidden reasoning or a new schema.

An instrumental response can occupy a phrase gap. A loud continuous motif should not unintentionally mask dense words. If a vocal hook/riff/chops carries the theme, instrumental parts support or deliberately contrast with it.

For chords and drum intro/drop/fill/break, use MUSIC_lib and SUNO_TAGS_lib. Global harmony/kit identity and local entries are separate. Medium risk does not authorize experimental inline chords; maximum Lyrics provides high-risk permission under SUNO_TAGS_lib unless explicitly restricted. A drum bridge/break label does not specify which voices/leads leave or return.

After rendering/compression, measure final Lyrics and Styles against both selected ceilings and hard caps (Lyrics 5000; Styles 1000) in UTF-16 under PARSER_spec. Keep protected words/endings; redesign conflicting instructions rather than truncate them. Word guidance and slider settings never replace the final count.
